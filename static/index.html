How it works
A three-agent pipeline built for clinical precision

Agent 1 — Extractor

Qwen2-VL running on AMD MI300X at full precision analyzes your scan and extracts detailed visual observations — structures, anomalies, densities, and locations.

Agent 2 — Analyst

Gemini processes the extracted observations alongside patient context to generate a structured clinical report with differential diagnosis, risk scoring, and staging.

Agent 3 — Validator

A second AI pass cross-checks the report for clinical consistency — verifying that findings, staging, and recommendations are coherent before presenting the result.
